FAQ

Questions, answered

How long does a website take to build?
Most marketing websites ship in 4–8 weeks. Larger web platforms take longer depending on scope — you get a firm timeline before we start.
Will my website be SEO-friendly?
Yes. Every build ships with clean semantic markup, fast Core Web Vitals, proper meta tags and structured data so it is ready to rank.
Do I own the website and code?
Completely. On final payment, all source code, design files and hosting access transfer to you — no lock-in.
Do you provide maintenance after launch?
Yes. We offer monthly maintenance covering updates, security, backups, monitoring and small feature work.
