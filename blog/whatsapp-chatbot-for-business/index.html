WhatsApp Chatbot for Business: How It Works & What It Costs

Published 2 October 2026 · by SRATECH

In India, customers don't want to fill a form or wait for an email — they message on WhatsApp and expect a reply now. A WhatsApp chatbot lets your business answer instantly, around the clock, without your team drowning in the same repeat questions. Here is exactly how it works, what it can do, and what it costs in 2026.

How a WhatsApp chatbot works

A WhatsApp chatbot is an automated assistant that sits on your business WhatsApp number. Built on the official WhatsApp Business API, it reads an incoming message, understands what the customer wants, and replies instantly using your own information — your products, pricing, timings and policies. When a conversation genuinely needs a person, it hands over cleanly to your team with the full chat history, so the customer never repeats themselves.

What it can automate

  • Answer FAQs instantly — timings, location, pricing, availability, delivery areas.
  • Capture leads — collect name, number and requirement, and log them to your CRM or sheet.
  • Order status & tracking — "where is my order?" answered in seconds.
  • Take orders & payments — share a catalogue, take an order, send a secure payment link.
  • Appointment booking & reminders — schedule slots and nudge before they arrive.
  • Notifications — order confirmations, shipping updates and offers (within WhatsApp's rules).

Why businesses in India use it

The payoff is simple: faster replies win more sales, every enquiry gets captured instead of lost, and your team stops answering the same question fifty times a day. A customer who gets an instant answer at 10 pm is far more likely to buy than one who waits until tomorrow.

Key idea: the bot is not there to replace your team — it clears the repetitive load so your people handle the conversations that actually need a human.

What a WhatsApp chatbot costs

There are two parts to the cost. First, the build — simple chatbot and workflow automations typically start alongside our Custom Web App package at ₹1,49,999, with larger AI-driven bots quoted after a short audit. Second, WhatsApp's own per-conversation charges through the official Business API, which are small and usage-based. We set it up so you only pay for what you use. See our AI automation service for how we approach it.

Rule-based vs AI chatbot

A simple rule-based bot follows menus and keywords — cheap and perfect for FAQs and order status. An AI chatbot understands natural language and answers from your content more flexibly, which suits businesses with varied questions. Many businesses start rule-based and add AI where it clearly helps.

How to set one up

Start with the five questions your customers ask most and automate just those, with a clean human-handover. Connect it to your catalogue or CRM, test it with real conversations, then expand. Keeping a human in the loop from day one is what keeps customers happy — automation should feel helpful, never like a wall.

A WhatsApp chatbot is an automated assistant on your business WhatsApp number that replies to customers instantly. Built on the WhatsApp Business API, it understands common questions, answers from your own information, captures leads, and hands over to a human when a conversation needs one — day or night.

Can a WhatsApp chatbot take orders and payments?

No — it removes the repetitive load. The bot handles the same routine questions that fill your day (timings, order status, pricing) and passes real conversations to your team with full context. Your people spend their time where it matters instead of copy-pasting the same reply.
