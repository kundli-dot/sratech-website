E-commerce Website Development in India (2026 Guide)

Published 2 October 2026 · by SRATECH

Selling online in India has never been bigger — or more competitive. A good online store does far more than list products: it loads fast, earns trust in seconds, makes checkout effortless, and keeps customers coming back. A weak one quietly loses sales at every step. This guide covers what e-commerce website development in India really costs in 2026, the features that matter, how to choose a platform, and how to launch a store that actually sells.

Must-have features for an online store

  • Product catalogue with categories, search and filters so buyers find items fast.
  • Cart & secure checkout that is short and mobile-friendly — most Indian shoppers buy on their phones.
  • Payment gateway supporting UPI, cards, net banking and wallets (Razorpay, PayU).
  • Order management & admin panel to process, track and update orders.
  • Notifications by email and WhatsApp for order confirmations and shipping updates.
  • Growth features — wishlists, coupons, order tracking and abandoned-cart recovery.

Which platform should you choose?

There is no single right answer — it depends on your scale and how custom your flow is.

WooCommerce / Shopify

Great for most small and growing brands: quick to launch, cost-effective and packed with plugins. Shopify charges a monthly fee; WooCommerce (on WordPress) is self-hosted and avoids that lock-in.

Custom-built store

When you need a custom checkout, unusual pricing or discount logic, deep integrations with your inventory or ERP, or marketplace features, a custom store on a modern stack gives you full control and no platform lock-in.

Tip: speed and trust sell. A store that loads in under 3 seconds, shows clear pricing and delivery info, and has an easy checkout will out-convert a prettier but slower one every time.

SEO & speed: don't skip them

An online store that no one can find does not sell. Every store should ship with clean URLs, product schema, fast Core Web Vitals, optimised images and proper meta tags — so your products can show up on Google. We build this in from day one rather than bolting it on later.

How to launch the right way

Start with your bestsellers and a clean, trustworthy design rather than trying to launch a thousand products at once. Get the checkout and payments rock-solid, add tracking and notifications, then expand the catalogue and marketing. A focused launch beats a bloated one.

Frequently asked questions

How much does an e-commerce website cost in India?
An e-commerce website in India starts at about ₹59,999 for a small store with up to 100 products, and around ₹99,999 for a growing brand with advanced filters, order tracking and multiple payment options. The final cost depends on product count, custom features, integrations and design.
Which platform is best for an online store in India?
For most small and growing brands, WooCommerce or Shopify is quick and cost-effective. For stores that need custom checkout, unusual pricing logic or deep integrations, a custom-built store on a modern stack gives you full control and no monthly platform lock-in. Choose based on scale and how custom your flow is.
What features does an e-commerce website need?
At minimum: a product catalogue with categories, search and filters, a cart and secure checkout, a payment gateway, order management and an admin panel. Growing stores add wishlists, order tracking, coupons, abandoned-cart recovery, email and WhatsApp notifications, and analytics.
Can customers pay online safely?
Yes. We integrate trusted Indian payment gateways such as Razorpay and PayU, which handle UPI, cards, net banking and wallets securely. Your store runs on HTTPS with SSL, and card details are handled by the gateway, never stored on your site.
How long does it take to build an online store?
A small store usually launches in 4–7 weeks, and a larger store with advanced features in 7–12 weeks. You get a firm timeline with milestones before we start, plus weekly demos so you can see the store come together.
