Website Development Cost in India (2026 Guide)

Published 20 September 2026 · by SRATECH

"How much does a website cost?" is the first question almost every business in India asks — and the honest answer is: it depends. A simple one-page site and a full e-commerce platform are both "websites", but they can differ in price by 50 times. That vagueness is exactly why so many business owners either overpay or end up with a cheap site that never brings a single enquiry. This guide gives you real, current 2026 numbers for website development in India, explains what actually drives the price up or down, and lists the extra costs that most agencies quietly leave out — so you can budget with clear eyes.

The prices below are SRATECH's actual published starting prices in Indian Rupees. Every studio is different, but these are realistic market figures you can use as a benchmark whether you build with us or anyone else.

What actually decides the price

Two "5-page websites" can cost ₹15,000 and ₹75,000 — and both quotes can be fair. The difference is in these five factors:

1. Number of pages

More pages mean more design and content work. A 5-page brochure site is far cheaper than a 25-page corporate site with service pages, case studies and a blog.

2. Design: template vs custom

A ready-made template is the cheapest route. A custom, made-for-your-brand design with a proper UX process costs more but looks unique and converts better. This is usually the single biggest price lever.

3. Features and functionality

A static information site is cheap. Add lead forms, WhatsApp integration, a blog/CMS, booking, login areas, dashboards or payment gateway integration, and each feature adds development time — and cost.

4. Content management (can you edit it yourself?)

If you want to update pages and publish blog posts yourself, you need a CMS like WordPress or a headless setup. That convenience adds a bit to the build but saves money long-term on small edits.

5. Integrations

Connecting your site to a CRM, payment gateway, analytics, SMS/WhatsApp APIs, or your existing software all adds scope. Each integration is extra engineering, so it shows up in the quote.

The extra costs no one mentions

The development fee is not the whole story. A trustworthy developer lists these separately instead of hiding them. Budget for:

  • Domain — your web address, paid yearly (usually a few hundred to ~₹1,500/year).
  • Hosting or cloud server — where your site lives, paid monthly or yearly.
  • Premium plugins or themes — some features need paid tools.
  • Paid APIs — maps, SMS, WhatsApp Business API and similar services charge usage.
  • Payment gateway charges — a small percentage per transaction (for e-commerce).
  • Maintenance — updates, security and backups, starting around ₹2,999/month for websites and ₹9,999/month for applications.
  • GST — where applicable.
Ask this before you pay: "Is this quote all-inclusive, and what will I pay separately?" A clear, itemised answer is one of the best signs of an honest developer.

How to budget for your website

A simple way to plan: decide what the website must do before you look at price. If it just needs to exist and look professional, a starter or business site is enough. If it must generate leads, add a proper CMS, strong SEO and lead forms. If it must sell, budget for e-commerce. If it runs part of your operations, that is a web application, not a website — and priced accordingly. Then add roughly 15–20% on top for the extra costs above, and always insist on an itemised quote so you know exactly what you are paying for.

One more rule: never choose on price alone. A ₹10,000 website that no customer can find on Google, or that you do not own, is more expensive than a ₹40,000 site that brings you enquiries for years.

Frequently asked questions

How much does website development cost in India in 2026?
A basic business website in India starts around ₹14,999, a professional multi-page site ranges from ₹49,999 to ₹79,999, an e-commerce store starts at about ₹59,999, and a custom web application starts around ₹1,49,999. The final cost depends on pages, design, features and integrations, so always get an itemised quote before you start.
Is a cheap website worth it?
A cheap template website can work for a very small business that just needs a basic online presence. But extremely low prices usually mean shared templates, slow load times, weak SEO and no ownership of the code. If the website is meant to generate leads or sales, invest in one that is fast, SEO-ready and fully yours.
Do I pay for a website once or monthly?
Development is usually a one-time project cost. On top of that you pay small recurring costs: a domain (yearly), hosting (monthly or yearly), and optional maintenance, which starts around ₹2,999 per month for websites. Payment gateway and API charges apply only when you use them.
What extra costs come with a website?
Beyond development, budget for a domain, hosting or a cloud server, premium plugins or themes, paid APIs, SMS or WhatsApp charges, payment gateway transaction fees, and GST where applicable. A good developer will list these separately and honestly instead of hiding them in one number.
How much does an e-commerce website cost in India?
An e-commerce website in India starts at about ₹59,999 for a small store with up to 100 products, and around ₹99,999 for a growing brand with advanced filters, order tracking and multiple payment options. Cost rises with product count, custom features and integrations.
Should I hire a freelancer or an agency?
A freelancer is cheaper and fine for a simple site, but availability and support can be uncertain. An agency or studio costs more but offers a team, structured process and after-launch support. For a business-critical website, the reliability and ownership terms usually matter more than saving a little upfront.
