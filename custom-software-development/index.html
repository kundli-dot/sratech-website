FAQ

Questions, answered

Why choose custom software over ready-made?
Ready-made tools force you to change how you work. Custom software fits your exact processes, removes manual steps and scales with you — often paying for itself in saved time.
How do you keep a custom build on time and budget?
We plan realistically, ship in iterations and demo weekly, so scope and cost stay visible. You approve the plan and the number before we start.
Will the software integrate with our existing tools?
Yes. Integrations are a core part of what we do — we connect your systems so data flows automatically instead of being re-keyed.
Do we own the source code?
Completely. On final payment all code and repository access transfer to you, with no licence fees or lock-in.
