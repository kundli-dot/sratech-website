FAQ

Questions, answered

Should I build native or cross-platform?
For most business apps, cross-platform (Flutter or React Native) is faster and more cost-effective. We recommend native only when performance or deep device features demand it.
How much does a mobile app cost in India?
It depends on scope — a focused MVP costs far less than a full-featured platform. We give a firm, itemised quote before starting, with no surprises.
Will you publish the app to the stores?
Yes. We handle App Store and Google Play submission, the review process and release on your accounts, which you fully own.
Do you maintain the app after launch?
Yes. We keep the app compatible with new OS versions and offer ongoing bug fixes and feature work.
