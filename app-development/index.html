FAQ

Questions, answered

How much does mobile app development cost in Delhi?

Final cost depends on screens, features and integrations, and you get a firm quote before we start.
Which is the best mobile app development company in Delhi?
There is no single top mobile app development company in Delhi for every project — the right partner ships stable Android and iOS apps, gives you full source-code ownership and supports you after launch. SRATECH does all three with transparent pricing and weekly demos, but always compare portfolios and support terms first.
How long does it take to build an app?
A simple Android app usually ships in 6–10 weeks. A full customer app for Android and iOS takes 10–16 weeks, and a marketplace or multi-vendor app takes longer depending on scope. You get a firm timeline with milestones before development begins, plus weekly demos.
Do you build Android and iOS apps?
Yes. We build native Android and native iOS apps, and cross-platform apps with Flutter and React Native that run on both from one codebase. We handle Play Store and App Store submission, and pick the approach that best fits your budget and performance needs.
Do I own the app source code?
Completely. On final payment, all source code, design files, and Play Store and App Store account access transfer to you. There is no vendor lock-in and no licence fee to us — the app is fully yours to run, update and extend.
Can you add a payment gateway to my app?
Yes. We integrate payment gateways such as Razorpay, Stripe and PayU for in-app payments, subscriptions and checkout, along with push notifications, chat, maps and other third-party APIs your mobile application development project needs.
Do you publish the app to the Play Store and App Store?
Yes. Our mobile app development services include Play Store and App Store deployment assistance — preparing store listings, screenshots and builds, and guiding you through Google and Apple review so your app goes live cleanly.
Do you maintain apps after launch?
Yes. Custom app maintenance starts at ₹9,999 per month and covers monitoring, security and backups, performance tuning, bug fixes, OS-update compatibility and small feature work, so your app keeps working as Android and iOS evolve.
