FAQ

Questions, answered

Which cloud provider should we use?
It depends on your stack, budget and team. We work across AWS, Azure and Google Cloud and recommend the best fit rather than a one-size-fits-all answer.
Can you lower our cloud costs?
Often, yes. Right-sizing resources, automation and better architecture typically cut waste — we assess your setup and show where savings are realistic.
What is CI/CD and why does it matter?
CI/CD automates building, testing and deploying your software, so releases are faster, safer and repeatable instead of manual and error-prone.
Do you provide ongoing DevOps support?
Yes. We offer monitoring, incident response and ongoing infrastructure work after the initial setup.
